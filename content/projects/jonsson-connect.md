I worked with a team of 4 other UT Dallas students to create Jonsson Connect, a cross-platform mobile app that serves as a one-stop-shop to learn about UT Dallas news, upcoming events, and jobs. I contributed to both the backend and frontend parts of the app. In the backend, I used Google Firebase to send push notifications when important changes occur in the database, such as the addition of a new UT Dallas event. In the frontend, I built many core features of the app such as a hamburger menu to navigate inside the app, and a rewards system that encourages UT Dallas alumni to attend more events. The Jonsson Connect mobile app was built using React Native.
