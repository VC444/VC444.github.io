My core principles:

  • Lead a balanced life
    • Doing meaningful, efficient work is integral to a fulfilling life.
    • Maintaining good physical and mental health is equally important.
  • Have high agency
    • The world can mold itself in a surprisingly positive way for you if you have high agency.
    • Just do things, and opportunities will present themselves.
    • As Jeff Bezos said, most decisions are two-way doors. If it goes wrong, you can just turn back.
    • "It's better to ask for forgiveness than permission" - Grace Hopper.
  • Have complete control over your emotions.
    • Bad events are only bad if you percieve them as bad.
    • Anger is the most useless emotion unless it's quickly turned into positive action.
  • Build and maintain strong relationships.
    • "Relationships are the currency of life". I'm not sure who said it, but I love it.
    • The relationship has to be genuine and not transactional.

One thought on AI - I think Jevon's Paradox will hold true when it comes to jobs and the general population.

  • As the cost of AI decreases, the demand for AI-based work goes up. This increases the number of people needed to do the work.
  • AI will be a force for good overall. It will not simply replace humans, but rather augment us and make us much more powerful.
