Storm's Edge is a fully-functional web app that mimics an online resort-reservation website. I worked on designing and building the backend APIs as well as the frontend website. I imagined the resort to be located in a place that is prone to thunderstorms, hence the name Storm's Edge. I also thought of beach's edge, forest's edge, and mountain's edge, but none of them had the same zing to it as Storm's Edge.
